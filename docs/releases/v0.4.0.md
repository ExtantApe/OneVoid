Works with Waycave 0.8.0, which replaces Waystation. Waystation is no longer recognized.

## What changed

- The hand-over event is `com.extantape.waycave.WaycaveHandOverEvent` and the landing service is `com.extantape.waycave.LandingService`. Same methods as before.
- `paper-plugin.yml` depends on `Waycave` (required, loaded before OneVoid). Messages, the startup note, `/onevoid status` and the README say Waycave.
- No config key changes.

## Installing over 0.3.0

Install Waycave 0.8.0 first, with Posse 0.11.1 and the rest as before. In Waycave's config keep `hand-over: true` on the OneBlock world and island posts off. The Multiverse-Inventories group that held the `waystation` world now needs the `waycave` world. With the server stopped, swap in `OneVoid-0.4.0.jar`.

## Tested

Unit tests: 17 pass. Bots with the real Waycave 0.8.0 jar (built from its waycave-080 branch at ea0917f) and the real Posse 0.11.1 jar, on throwaway Paper servers: Paper 26.2, the full suite, 90 of 90 passed, including the `crashes` section that failed in 0.3.0. Paper 26.3, server-only checks, 5 of 5 passed (bots can't play there). Not tested: Paper 26.1.2, a Waycave other than 0.8.0 at that commit, a real game client.

The test harness waits for Waycave's "cave is built" line before it reads the gates, because Waycave lays the cave out again once the OneBlock world is up.
